# Case_Study_Fashionwave

Retail Sales Performance Analyst (Case Study) | Power BI , Excel
• Analyzed 15 months of transactional data across 20 retail stores to evaluate margin health and track key metrics, managing a dataset with ₹68.15 Cr in revenue and 100K+ transactions.
• Identified critical margin erosion by building a promotional discount impact model, proving high discount tiers (15–20%) degraded margins to 27.24% without driving incremental revenue.
• Uncovered a 42% revenue disparity between weekday and weekend performance, delivering targeted data recommendations to capture an untapped +15% weekend revenue goal.
• Segmented 3 distinct customer profiles (Returning, New, Unknown) to map ₹37 Cr in anchor revenue and formulated data-driven retention frameworks.
